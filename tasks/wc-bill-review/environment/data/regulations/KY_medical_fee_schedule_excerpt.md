# Kentucky Workers' Compensation Medical Fee Schedule (excerpt)

803 KAR 25:089-S, as amended. Provisions on payment calculation. Citations and amounts are reproduced for claims staff training and are not the official text.

## Section 3. Professional services

(1) The maximum payment for a professional service listed with relative value units is the relative value units multiplied by the professional conversion factor in effect on the date the service was performed. A service listed with a flat fee is paid that fee for each unit.

(2) A payer shall pay the lesser of the provider's charge for the service or the maximum payment under this schedule, unless a written network agreement between the payer and the provider sets a lower rate, in which case the agreement rate applies to services performed while the provider participates in that network.

(3) Conversion factors are adopted by order of the commissioner. A published proposal has no effect unless it is adopted.

## Section 5. Surgery and procedures

(1) When more than one procedure with a multiple procedure indicator of 2 is performed for the same injured worker by the same provider on the same date, the procedure with the highest maximum payment is paid at 100 percent and each additional procedure at 50 percent of its maximum payment. The ranking is made on the maximum payments, regardless of the order in which procedures are listed, the presence or placement of modifier 51, or whether the procedures were submitted on one bill or several.

(2) A procedure with a bilateral indicator of 1 billed with modifier 50 on a single line is paid at 150 percent of its maximum payment before any multiple procedure reduction.

(3) An assistant surgeon, identified by modifier 80, is paid 16 percent of the maximum payment for the procedure. Assistant surgeon services are not payable for procedures whose assistant surgeon indicator is 0.

## Section 6. Correct coding

(1) The commissioner adopts the procedure edit table. When a column 1 code and its column 2 code are billed for the same injured worker by the same provider for the same date of service, on the same bill or on separate bills, the column 2 code is not separately payable.

(2) Where the edit's modifier indicator is 1, the column 2 code is separately payable when it is billed with modifier 59 or XS. Where the indicator is 0, no modifier allows separate payment.

## Section 8. Hospital outpatient and ambulatory surgical facility services

(1) A facility service listed in the facility schedule is paid its relative weight multiplied by the facility conversion factor in effect on the date of service, for each unit.

(2) Services with status indicator T are subject to a multiple procedure discount when more than one is reported on the same facility bill: the service with the highest payment is paid at 100 percent and every other T service at 50 percent.

(3) Services with status indicator N, and charges reported under revenue codes 037X (anesthesia) and 071X (recovery room), are packaged into the payment for the procedure and are not separately payable.

(4) Pharmacy (025X) and medical-surgical supply (027X) charges reported without a procedure code are paid at 65 percent of billed charges.

(5) The lesser-of rule in Section 3(2) applies to facility services.

## Section 11. Services rendered outside Kentucky

Medical services rendered outside Kentucky to a worker whose claim is under the Kentucky Act are paid under this schedule.
