# Medical Bill Review Procedures — Claims Operations Manual (excerpt)

Chapter 9. Processing provider bills

9.1 Compensability. We pay treatment only for body parts accepted on the claim. Body part determinations can change as a claim develops; the current determination for a body part is the one in effect now. A service is related to the body part of the diagnosis it primarily treats. On a CMS-1500, that is the first diagnosis listed for the line; on a UB-04, it is the principal diagnosis of the bill. Services dated before the date of injury are not related to the claim.

9.2 Claim frequency. Providers report a claim frequency code on each bill. Code 1 is an original bill. Code 7 replaces a bill we already received, identified by the original reference number; the replacement supersedes the earlier bill entirely, including any earlier replacement. Code 8 voids a bill we already received; neither the voided bill nor the void notice is payable.

9.3 Duplicates. A resubmission of a bill that is still active, with the same patient, provider and service lines, is a duplicate and is not payable. A new original bill sent after a void is not a duplicate of the voided bill.

9.4 Prior payments. Before paying a service, credit every payment we have already issued for that same service, meaning the same claim, provider, date of service and procedure or revenue code, on any bill that has not been voided. A reversal entry cancels the payment it reverses. Payments on voided bills are recovered from the provider separately and are not credited. We never pay less than zero on a line; overpayments are handled through recovery.

9.5 The amount still payable on a line is the allowed amount less the credited payments.
