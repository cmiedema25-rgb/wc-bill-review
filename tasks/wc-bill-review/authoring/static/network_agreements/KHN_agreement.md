# Keystone Health Network — Workers' Compensation Payor Agreement (excerpt)

Article 4. Reimbursement

4.1 Participating providers. This Article applies to covered services rendered by a provider during the provider's period of participation in the Network, as recorded in the Network's participation roster. Services rendered before a provider's participation begins or after it terminates are not subject to this Agreement.

4.2 Contract rate. The contract rate for a covered service depends on the state whose workers' compensation law governs the claim:

(a) Kentucky and Idaho claims: the amount allowable under the applicable state fee schedule, reduced by ten percent (10%).

(b) New Mexico and South Carolina claims: the provider's billed charges, reduced by ten percent (10%).

4.3 Payment. Payor shall reimburse the lesser of (i) the provider's billed charges, (ii) the amount allowable under the applicable state fee schedule, and (iii) the contract rate. Nothing in this Agreement requires payment of a service that is not compensable under the claim or not payable under the state fee schedule rules.

4.4 The Network's discount applies to professional and facility services alike.
