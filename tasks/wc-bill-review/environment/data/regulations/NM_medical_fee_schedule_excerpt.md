# New Mexico Workers' Compensation Health Care Provider Fee Schedule (excerpt)

11.4.7 NMAC-S, Parts 8 through 12. Reproduced for claims staff training; not the official text.

## 11.4.7.8 Maximum allowable payment, practitioners

A. The maximum allowable payment (MAP) for a practitioner service is the listed relative value multiplied by the New Mexico professional conversion factor applicable to the date of service. Items listed with a flat amount are paid that amount per unit.

B. Payment is the lesser of the billed charge and the MAP. A payer and a provider may agree in writing to a lower amount; the agreed amount applies only to services rendered during the term of the agreement as to that provider.

C. Conversion factor changes take effect on the effective date stated in the adoption order. Proposed factors circulated for comment are not effective.

## 11.4.7.9 Multiple and bilateral procedures

A. Where a single provider performs two or more procedures carrying multiple procedure indicator 2 on one patient on the same date, the procedure with the greatest MAP is reimbursed at 100 percent, the procedure with the second greatest MAP at 50 percent, and each remaining procedure at 25 percent of MAP. Modifier 51 and line sequence are informational only and do not determine the ranking. Procedures submitted on separate bills for the same date are ranked together.

B. Bilateral procedures (bilateral indicator 1) billed on one line with modifier 50: 150 percent of MAP, before any multiple procedure reduction.

C. Assistant at surgery (modifier 80): 20 percent of MAP for the procedure, payable only where the schedule's assistant surgeon indicator is 1.

## 11.4.7.10 Correct coding initiative

Paired codes in the adopted edit table are not separately reimbursable when the column 2 code is billed with its column 1 code for the same patient by the same provider on the same date of service, whether or not on the same bill. For pairs with modifier indicator 1, modifier 59 or XS on the column 2 code allows separate reimbursement; for pairs with indicator 0 it does not.

## 11.4.7.11 Outpatient facilities

A. Facility services listed in the facility schedule: relative weight times the facility conversion factor applicable to the date of service, per unit.

B. Status T services on one facility bill: highest-paying at 100 percent, all others at 50 percent.

C. Status N items, anesthesia (revenue codes 037X) and recovery room (071X) charges are bundled into the procedure and not paid separately.

D. Pharmacy (025X) and supplies (027X) without a procedure code: 55 percent of billed charges.

E. Payment is the lesser of billed charges and the amount computed under this section, subject to 11.4.7.8 B.

## 11.4.7.12 Out-of-state providers

Services rendered outside New Mexico are reimbursed under the fee schedule of the state where the service was rendered, applying that state's conversion factors and payment rules, when that state is Kentucky, South Carolina or Idaho. Services rendered in any other state are reimbursed under this rule. The place of service is the service facility location reported on the bill.
