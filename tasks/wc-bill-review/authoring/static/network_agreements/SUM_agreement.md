# Summit Occupational PPO — Participating Payor Agreement (excerpt)

Schedule B. Compensation

B-1. Scope. Schedule B governs services furnished by Summit participating providers on dates of service within each provider's participation term. A provider whose participation has ended is not a participating provider for later dates of service, whatever other records may show.

B-2. Rate. For claims governed by the New Mexico or South Carolina workers' compensation acts, the Summit rate is eighty-eight percent (88%) of the fee schedule allowance. For claims governed by the Kentucky or Idaho acts, the Summit rate is eighty-eight percent (88%) of the provider's billed charge.

B-3. The payor pays the least of the billed charge, the fee schedule allowance and the Summit rate. All fee schedule rules (edits, multiple procedure reductions, packaging, and limits on services rendered out of state) are applied before the Summit rate is computed.
