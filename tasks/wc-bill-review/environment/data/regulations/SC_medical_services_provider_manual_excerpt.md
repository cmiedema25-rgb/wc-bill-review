# South Carolina Workers' Compensation Commission Medical Services Provider Manual (excerpt)

2026 edition, Chapters 2, 4 and 7. Reproduced for claims staff training; not the official text.

## Chapter 2. Payment principles

2.1 Reimbursement for a professional service is the lesser of the provider's charge or the maximum allowable payment. The maximum allowable payment for a service with relative value units is the RVUs multiplied by the conversion factor in effect on the date of service; supplies and devices with a listed fee are paid the listed fee per unit.

2.2 Network agreements. An employer or carrier may reimburse at a lower contracted rate under a written agreement with the provider in effect on the date of service.

2.3 Only conversion factors adopted by the Commission are used. A proposed factor is not used for any date of service.

## Chapter 4. Surgery

4.1 Multiple procedures. For procedures with a multiple procedure indicator of 2 performed by the same provider for the same patient on the same date of service, reimbursement is 100 percent of the maximum allowable payment for the procedure with the highest maximum allowable payment and 50 percent for each other procedure. The order of procedures on the bill, modifier 51, and the number of bills submitted do not change the ranking.

4.2 Bilateral procedures. Procedures with bilateral indicator 1 billed on one line with modifier 50 are reimbursed at 150 percent of the maximum allowable payment before the multiple procedure reduction.

4.3 Assistant surgeons (modifier 80) receive 16 percent of the maximum allowable payment where the assistant surgeon indicator is 1, and nothing where it is 0.

4.4 Edits. The Commission follows the adopted procedure edit table. A column 2 code is not reimbursed when billed with its column 1 code for the same patient, provider and date of service, on one bill or more. Modifier 59 or XS on the column 2 code allows reimbursement only for pairs with modifier indicator 1.

## Chapter 7. Facility services

7.1 Ambulatory surgical and hospital outpatient services are reimbursed at the relative weight multiplied by the facility conversion factor in effect on the date of service, per unit, or billed charges if less.

7.2 When more than one status T service appears on a facility bill, the highest-paying service is reimbursed at 100 percent and the rest at 50 percent.

7.3 Status N services and charges under revenue codes 037X and 071X are packaged and not separately reimbursed.

7.4 Pharmacy (025X) and supply (027X) charges with no procedure code are reimbursed at 60 percent of billed charges.

7.5 Out-of-state services. For services rendered in Kentucky, New Mexico or Idaho, reimbursement is the lesser of the amount under this manual and the amount under the fee schedule and rules of the state where the service was rendered. Services rendered in any other state are reimbursed under this manual. The place of service is the service facility location on the bill.
