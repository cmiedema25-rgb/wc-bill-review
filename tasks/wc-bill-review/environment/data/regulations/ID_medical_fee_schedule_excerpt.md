# Idaho Industrial Commission Medical Fee Schedule (excerpt)

IDAPA 17.02.09-S, Rule 031. Reproduced for claims staff training; not the official text.

## 031.01 Acceptable charge

a. The acceptable charge for a professional service is the relative value units listed for Idaho multiplied by the conversion factor in effect on the date of service. Listed flat amounts are acceptable charges per unit.

b. The payor shall pay the lesser of the provider's billed charge and the acceptable charge, or a lower amount under a written preferred provider agreement in effect on the date of service.

c. Conversion factors take effect only upon adoption by the Commission. Proposed factors under public review are not acceptable charges.

## 031.02 Multiple, bilateral and assistant surgery

a. Two or more procedures with multiple procedure indicator 2 performed by one provider on the same date for the same claimant: the procedure with the highest acceptable charge at 100 percent, the next at 50 percent, and the third and later procedures at 25 percent. Rank by acceptable charge, not by bill sequence or modifier 51, and include procedures billed on separate bills for that date.

b. Bilateral procedures (indicator 1) with modifier 50 on one line: 150 percent of the acceptable charge, before the multiple procedure reduction.

c. Assistant surgeon (modifier 80): 20 percent of the acceptable charge where the assistant surgeon indicator is 1; not payable where it is 0.

## 031.03 Edits

A column 2 code of the adopted edit table billed with its column 1 code by the same provider for the same claimant and date of service, on any number of bills, is not payable. Modifier 59 or XS overrides the edit only where the modifier indicator is 1.

## 031.04 Facilities

a. Outpatient facility services: relative weight times the facility conversion factor in effect on the date of service, per unit, or billed charges if less.

b. Multiple status T services on one facility bill: highest at 100 percent, others at 50 percent.

c. Status N services and revenue codes 037X and 071X are packaged, not separately payable.

d. Pharmacy (025X) and supplies (027X) without a procedure code: 70 percent of billed charges.

## 031.05 Services outside Idaho

Services provided outside Idaho to a claimant under the Idaho Act are paid under this fee schedule.
