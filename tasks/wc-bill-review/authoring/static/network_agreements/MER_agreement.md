# Meridian Select — Workers' Compensation Network Services Agreement (excerpt)

Section 6. Pricing

6.1 Meridian pricing applies to services rendered on or after a provider's effective date of participation and on or before its termination date, if any.

6.2 Except as stated in 6.3, the Meridian price is the state fee schedule allowance less seven percent (7%).

6.3 For claims under the South Carolina Workers' Compensation Law, the Meridian price is the billed charge less seven percent (7%).

6.4 The amount payable is the lowest of the billed charge, the fee schedule allowance and the Meridian price.
